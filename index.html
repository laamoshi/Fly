About Us – EthioFly

ETHIOFLY is a professionally developed travel and flight information website proudly owned and operated in Ethiopia.

Our mission is to simplify air travel for Ethiopian and international travelers by providing reliable, transparent, and up-to-date travel information in one convenient platform.

EthioFly was founded by Khalid Abdi Aydid with the vision of creating a locally owned digital travel solution that meets international standards.

The platform was designed and developed by Team Ali Abdi Abib, a skilled development team dedicated to building secure, fast, and user-friendly web solutions.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is EthioFly?

EthioFly is a trusted flight comparison and travel platform designed for Ethiopian travelers.

Does EthioFly sell flight tickets?

No. EthioFly does not currently sell tickets directly. Our booking and payment system is under development.

Is EthioFly free to use?

Yes. EthioFly is completely free for all users.

How does EthioFly make money?

  • Advertising (Google Ads)
  • Affiliate partnerships
  • Sponsored travel deals

Is my personal information safe?

Yes. Please review our Privacy Policy for full details.
