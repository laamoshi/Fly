About Ethiofly

Who We Are

Ethiofly is a global flight search and booking platform that helps travelers easily find, compare, and book affordable airline tickets across Africa, Europe, Asia, and the Middle East.

We operate as a modern travel technology platform, combining real-time flight data with a user-friendly booking experience to make travel planning simple, fast, and cost-effective.

What We Do

Ethiofly provides powerful tools that allow users to:

  • Search and compare flights from multiple airlines and travel partners
  • Access real-time pricing powered by global flight APIs
  • Book flights directly or securely connect to trusted airline and partner platforms
  • Discover the best flight deals across international routes

Our system integrates with leading travel technology providers to ensure accurate pricing, availability, and seamless booking experiences.

How Ethiofly Works

Ethiofly combines three booking methods to serve different user needs:

1. Direct Booking

Users can book flights directly through our platform using secure online payment methods.

2. Partner Redirection

For certain routes and deals, users are redirected to trusted airline or travel partners to complete their booking.

3. Assisted Booking

Customers can contact our team via phone or WhatsApp for personalized booking support and travel assistance.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is EthioFly?

EthioFly is a trusted flight comparison and travel platform designed for Ethiopian travelers. We help users compare flights, hotels, travel packages, and deals from reliable third-party providers so they can make informed travel decisions.

Does EthioFly sell flight tickets?

Currently, EthioFly does not sell flight tickets directly. Our ticket booking and payment system is under development.

Is EthioFly free to use?

Yes. EthioFly is completely free for all users. There are no registration or comparison fees.

Are the prices shown on EthioFly accurate?

Prices are provided by third-party airlines, hotels, and booking partners. While we strive to display the most up-to-date information, prices and availability may change at any time depending on the provider.

How does EthioFly make money?

  • Advertising (such as Google Ads)
  • Affiliate partnerships with airlines, hotels, and travel providers
  • Sponsored travel deals

Can I book hotels and travel packages?

Yes. EthioFly allows users to compare hotels and travel packages and then redirects them to trusted third-party providers to complete the booking.

Is my personal information safe?

Yes. Protecting your privacy is very important to us. EthioFly follows strict data protection and security standards. For more details, please review our Privacy Policy.

💳 Refund & Cancellation Policy – ETHIOFLY.COM

1. General Policy

ETHIOFLY is an Online Travel Agency (OTA). All flight tickets, refunds, cancellations, and changes are governed primarily by the rules and conditions of the respective airline.

By booking through ETHIOFLY, customers agree to be bound by both airline fare rules and ETHIOFLY administrative policies.

2. Refund Eligibility

  • Refund eligibility is determined strictly by airline fare rules
  • Some tickets may be non-refundable or partially refundable
  • ETHIOFLY does not guarantee that all tickets are refundable
  • Refund requests must be submitted via email support or the website refund request form

3. ETHIOFLY Administrative Fees

In addition to airline penalties, ETHIOFLY applies the following service fees:

Refund Processing Fee

USD 35 fixed administrative fee per ticket (where refund is permitted by the airline).

Cancellation Fee

USD 20 administrative fee per ticket, plus any airline-imposed penalties.

Date & Time Changes

  • Subject to airline fare rules and availability
  • USD 20 administrative fee per change
  • Plus any airline fare difference and airline change penalties

4. Processing Time

Approved refunds are processed within 7 to 14 business days. Processing time may vary depending on the airline and payment processor.

5. Payment Method for Refunds

  • Refunds are issued only to the original payment method used at booking
  • Bank transfers or alternative methods are not supported unless required by law

6. No-Show Policy

If a passenger fails to show up for a scheduled flight without prior cancellation, the booking will be treated as a no-show.

No-show refunds, if any, are governed strictly by airline rules. ETHIOFLY administrative fees will still apply where applicable.

7. Name Changes

Name changes are governed strictly by airline policies. Some airlines do not allow name changes after ticket issuance.

8. Special Circumstances

Medical cases, death, or force majeure situations are handled strictly according to airline rules.

ETHIOFLY does not waive administrative fees unless required by airline policy or law.

9. Chargebacks & Disputes

  • Customers should contact ETHIOFLY support before initiating a chargeback
  • Unnotified chargebacks may result in processing delays
  • ETHIOFLY reserves the right to provide booking and communication records to banks and card networks
